# FleetOS — Fleet Operator Standards & Chauffeur SLA Handbook

**Handbook Version:** 1.0.0  
**Enforcement Authority:** FleetOS Quality & Standards Council  
**Jurisdiction:** UAE, KSA, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman

---

## 1. Fleet Vehicle Certification & Maintenance Standards

All vehicles listed on the FleetOS inter-fleet exchange must meet the following mandatory specifications prior to receiving active clearinghouse dispatch credentials:

### 1.1 Age & Mileage Limitations
- **First Class / VIP / Armored**: Maximum vehicle age of **≤ 36 months** from initial registration; odometer `< 100,000 km`.
- **Executive Sedan**: Maximum vehicle age of **≤ 48 months**; odometer `< 140,000 km`.
- **Mandatory Inspections**: Bi-annual 50-point mechanical and aesthetic inspection signed by a certified dealer (Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Al Tayer, Juffali).

### 1.2 Required Onboard Executive Amenities
Every vehicle dispatched under FleetOS enterprise contracts must carry:
1. Glass bottled mineral water (Evian, San Pellegrino, or Al Ain Zero Glass) chilled to 6°C–8°C.
2. Fast-charging high-speed cables (USB-C, Lightning, MagSafe wireless puck).
3. Complimentary high-speed 5G WiFi hotspot.
4. Individually wrapped luxury sanitizing wipes & tissue boxes.
5. Daily sanitized leather upholstery with zero lingering odors or heavy artificial fragrances.

## 2. Chauffeur Protocol & Code of Conduct

### 2.1 Professional Attire & Grooming
- Dark charcoal or black tailored two-piece suit with a pressed white collared dress shirt and conservative silk tie.
- Polished black formal oxford dress shoes.
- Strict personal hygiene and immaculate grooming standards.

### 2.2 In-Trip Protocol
- **The "Silent Executive" Mandate**: The chauffeur must never initiate unsolicited personal conversation. If the executive initiates conversation, the chauffeur responds with courteous brevity.
- **Privacy & Non-Disclosure**: Chauffeurs are strictly bound by confidentiality regarding passenger identities, telephone conversations, travel destinations, and itinerary details.
- **Flight & Tarmac Greetings**: For airport pickups, the chauffeur must stand prominently at the designated arrival gate 15 minutes before customs clearance holding an electronic high-resolution iPad name board.

## 3. SLA Penalties, Default Matrix & Escrow Forfeiture

| Violation Category | Definition / Threshold | Platform Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Late Arrival (> 10 min)** | Driver arrives after scheduled/flight-adjusted time without prior notice. | **50% Fare Refund** to client; charged to operator escrow. |
| **No-Show / Unreported Miss** | Driver fails to service booking without 2 hours prior emergency notification. | **100% Fare Forfeit + $250 Penalty** + 14-day exchange suspension. |
| **Unauthorized Vehicle Downgrade** | Dispatched vehicle belongs to a lower tier than booked (e.g. E-Class instead of S-Class). | **Automatic 50% Fare Adjustment** + Tier strike. |
| **Aesthetic / Cleanliness Failure** | Dirty exterior, smoke odor, or missing amenities reported with photo proof. | **$75 Quality Credit** issued to client at operator expense. |

---

## 4. Escrow Clearing & Payout Timeline
1. **Trip Execution**: Ride completed and confirmed via chauffeur telematics and passenger sign-off.
2. **2-Hour Dispute Window**: Corporate client has 2 hours to lodge any service complaints or tier discrepancies.
3. **Automated Settlement**: Escrow releases 85%–88% net earnings directly to the servicing operator's nominated GCC bank account or virtual corporate IBAN within **24 hours**.
